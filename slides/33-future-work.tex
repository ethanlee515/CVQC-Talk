  \note{
    \footnotesize
    Two directions to close. First, weaken the cryptographic assumptions,
    aiming to replace the classical-client QFHE route used in this talk
    by one based on quantum-secure trapdoor claw-free functions.
    Second, survey the multiparty quantum computation literature for
    applications of the protocol and its preparation, factorization,
    and authenticated-distillation techniques. Present this as an
    exploration of possible applications, without promising an
    immediate composable MPQC construction.
  }
