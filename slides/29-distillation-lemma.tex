\begin{frame}{Distillation: One Bad Input}
  \centering
  \setbeamertemplate{theorems}[normal font]
  \setbeamercolor{block title}{fg=white,bg=CVQCInk}
  \setbeamercolor{block body}{fg=CVQCInk,bg=CVQCFog}
  \begin{tikzpicture}
    \path[use as bounding box] (-6.6,-3.15) rectangle (6.6,3.05);
    \node[inner sep=0pt] at (0,0.30) {%
      \begin{minipage}{12.2cm}
        \begin{lemma}[Informal]
          \par\vspace*{0.65em}
          \centering
          On acceptance,
          {\large
          \[
            \begin{gathered}
              1\times\text{factorizable attack}
                +3\times\text{ciphertext-level attack}\\[0.65em]
              \longmapsto\quad\text{ciphertext-level attack}.
            \end{gathered}
          \]
          }
          \par\vspace*{0.35em}
        \end{lemma}
      \end{minipage}%
    };
    \uncover<2->{
      \node[font=\large\bfseries,text=CVQCTeal,inner sep=0pt] at (0,-2.35)
        {Plaintext-level attacks are distilled away.};
    }
  \end{tikzpicture}

  \note{
    \footnotesize
    This is the one-bad-input transition within the paper's joint
    factorizable invariant. Three copies have no plaintext-level
    modification, and the fourth may have one arbitrary local
    plaintext-level operator beneath its pad. A shared, potentially
    global ciphertext-level operation may already correlate the copies
    and the prover's memory; the counts do not assume independent
    ciphertext attacks. For any choice of the bad copy, the accepted
    output has no remaining plaintext-level attack. The ciphertext
    operation outside its uniform live pad is independent of the secret
    pad and permutation keys. The argument carries all branch weights
    and respects the invariant's aggregate trace bounds; it does not
    assert that a single block always accepts. At the local comparison
    level, the honest reduction has the same normalized surviving state
    whenever the real block accepts and can only increase acceptance
    probability. The zero-bad case is included by taking the plaintext
    operator to be identity. Source: one-bad-input reduction guarantee,
    accepted one-bad instrument, and iterative invariant propagation.
  }
\end{frame}
